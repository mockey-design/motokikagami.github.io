Ongoing research

Viewpoint-Assisted Selection in VR

In progress · Working title

Multi-Crossing: Progressive Multi-Selection through Automated Viewpoint Recommendation in VR

Selecting a target set in a dense 3D scene can require multiple viewpoints. This project combines automated viewpoint recommendation with progressive selection, helping users build a selection across views.

Progress: Study 1 (18 participants) and Study 2 (24 participants) are complete and analyzed. A third study is planned to compare Lasso and Crossing selection with manual and automated viewpoints.

My role: I lead the project and independently carry out the implementation, study design, data collection, analysis, and writing, in collaboration with Francisco R. Ortega (Colorado State University), Mayra Donaji Barrera Machuca (University of Calgary), and Robert J. Teather (Monash University).
